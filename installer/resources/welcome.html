Welcome to TagoClip

TagoClip is a soft clipper for 808s and drums: the classic FL soft clip curve as a plugin, with optional oversampling against aliasing, plus hard and tanh curves, delta listening and mono low end.

This installer lets you choose which plugin formats to install:

  • VST3 — FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper and most other DAWs
  • Audio Unit (AU) — Logic Pro and GarageBand

If you are not sure, install both.
